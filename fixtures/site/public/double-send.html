Double send

Sends one GA4 purchase image hit for the fake tag G-PWTEST0001 with the fake client ID 555000111.1700000001, then 300 ms later the embedded frame /double-send-frame sends the same hit. With mode=top the page sends its hit only and embeds no frame. With mode=listener one purchase listener is attached twice, so one event pushes the same purchase twice and sends two hits from this frame. With mode=one the page pushes purchase once and sends two equal hits. With mode=different it sends two hits that differ in one param.
